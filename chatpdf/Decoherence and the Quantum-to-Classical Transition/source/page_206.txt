The interpretation of the terms appearing on the right-hand side of (5.57)

the master equation (see Sect. 5.2.2). The ﬁrst term is the usual Liouville–

    Equation (5.57) is commonly known as the Caldeira–Leggett master equa-
tion (here written for the case of a central harmonic-oscillator system), which
was ﬁrst derived by Caldeira and Leggett in the early 1980s using a path-
integral approach [221]. It is widely used in the modeling of decoherence and
dissipation processes. In fact, it has often been quite successfully applied to
situations in which the assumptions on which the derivation of the equation
is based were not strictly fulﬁlled (for example, in quantum-optical settings
where often kB T  Λ [222]).
