From (3.40) we see that F (x − x ) plays the role of a localization rate, i.e.,

between two positions x and x become locally suppressed.
    Equations (3.40) and (3.41) represent our main result. We have found the
expression that quantiﬁes the inﬂuence of a collection of incoming particles
scattering oﬀ our object of interest on the interference (oﬀ-diagonal) terms
in the position density matrix of the object. As we have not yet speciﬁed the
