Provided the spatial separation between the center-of-mass states |P0  and
|P1  is suﬃciently macroscopic, we have therefore generated a state of the
Schrödinger-cat type. Here, the resonator plays the role of the cat, while the
Cooper-pair box corresponds to the unstable atom in Schrödinger’s original
setting.

additional control pulse to the Cooper-pair box after a certain delay time,
