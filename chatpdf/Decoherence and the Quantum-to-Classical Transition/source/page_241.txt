Dynamics of the Model

Let us now evaluate the above integrals (5.166) for two important limiting
situations. The ﬁrst case, Δ0  sN , corresponds to weak intrinsic dynamics of
the system. In this limit, the dynamics of the system will hardly be inﬂuenced
by the presence of the self-Hamiltonian of the system. Thus we expect our
model to approach the results of the static model discussed in Sect. 2.10.
