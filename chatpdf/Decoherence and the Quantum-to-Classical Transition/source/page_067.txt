     Inspection of the right-hand side of (2.54) shows that the ﬁnal state of the
system–apparatus combination is in general described by an entangled state,
i.e., we can no longer attribute an individual state vector to the system or the
apparatus. Entanglement has thus been created dynamically. The superposi-
tion initially present only in the system has been ampliﬁed to the level of the
(typically macroscopic) apparatus, in the sense that the ﬁnal superposition
involves both the system and the apparatus.
