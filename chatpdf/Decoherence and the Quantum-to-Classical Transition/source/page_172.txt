4.2.2 Derivation of the Born–Markov Master Equation

In this section, we shall derive the Born–Markov master equation (4.8) from
ﬁrst principles. Readers are encouraged to work through the main steps of
the derivation, in particular in order to get a better understanding of the
assumptions that enter into this derivation.

Interaction-Picture Description

in the usual form as
                            Ĥ = ĤS + ĤE + Ĥint .                  (4.12)
