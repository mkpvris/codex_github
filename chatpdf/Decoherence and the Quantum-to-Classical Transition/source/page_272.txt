6.2.2 Experimental Setup

Let us now outline the experimental setup used by the Zeilinger group for
demonstrating the wave nature of massive molecules. The original experi-
ment, reported in 1999 in a Nature article by Markus Arndt and cowork-
ers [263], used C60 molecules, often nicknamed “buckey balls” because of
their shape (see Fig. 6.7). Subsequent experiments, results of which were ﬁrst
reported in a 2002 paper by the Zeilinger group [264], were carried out using
even bigger C70 molecules (in the shape of an elongated buckey ball) and a
diﬀerent experimental setup [264–267]. Our following discussion will be based
on this later series of experiments.
