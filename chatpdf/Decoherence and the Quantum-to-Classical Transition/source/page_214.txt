    A crucial feature of the time evolution of Aint as given by (5.77) is the
direct dependence on the initial conditions, in particular, on the initial sep-
arations X0 and P0 . The dependence enters via the coeﬃcients κx and κp ,
which at time t = 0 take the values κx = P0 and κp = X0 [see (5.73b)].
As we shall see now, this dependence is responsible for the very diﬀerent
timescales for decoherence in position and momentum as mentioned above
(see also Fig. 5.4).
    First, let us focus on the limiting case (5.66) in which the initial state
corresponds to a superposition of two Gaussians separated in position space
only. Since κp = X0 at t = 0, we see that the contribution from the normal-
diﬀusion term 4Dκ2p in the evolution equation (5.77) for Aint immediately
plays a role in damping interference.
