ence on the one hand and purely collective decoherence on the other hand,
delineate the limits on the size of a DFS. For the spin–boson model, in the
limit of collective decoherence (and many qubits), the dimension of the DFS
will asymptotically approach the dimension of the original Hilbert space,
thus allowing for optimal decoherence-free encoding. In the opposite limit
of independent decoherence, however, we shall see that there exists no DFS
that would allow for an encoding of logical qubits. We shall not reproduce
the detailed models and proofs necessary to establish these results, but we
nonetheless would like to gain some intuition into the relationship between
the size of the DFS and the particular decoherence model. This will be done
in the following.
