Fig. 5.4. Time evolution of a superposition of two Gaussian wave packets in the
Wigner picture, as studied by Paz, Habib, and Zurek [184]. The left column cor-
responds to a separation of the initial wave packets in position only, whereas the
right column represents the dynamics of a superposition of wave packets separated
in momentum only. Interference between the two wave packets is represented by
oscillations between the direct peaks. The interaction with the environment damps
these oscillations, a process which represents decoherence. However, this damping
occurs on diﬀerent timescales for the two initial conditions.
