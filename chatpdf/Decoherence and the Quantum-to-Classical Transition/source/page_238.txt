Solving the Model

Despite the presence of the tunneling term, the global dynamics of the model
deﬁned by (5.150) can be solved exactly, as shown by Dobrovitski et al.
[243] and Cucchietti, Paz, and Zurek [118]. We shall closely follow the latter
approach [118] in our subsequent derivation.
