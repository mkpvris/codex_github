We now see that we have correlated each of the four possibilities Ẑ000 , Ẑ100 ,
Ẑ010 , and Ẑ001 with a distinct ancilla state |ij, (i, j) ∈ {0, 1}. Since these
states are mutually orthogonal, we can perfectly distinguish the four error
types by measuring the two ancilla qubits.
