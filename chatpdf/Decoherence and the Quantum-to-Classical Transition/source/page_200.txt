This form is similar to the diﬀusion equation (5.39) in terms of the appearance

comparison with the normal-diﬀusion term (5.34). In such cases, we may use
a simpliﬁed version of the master equation (5.25) without the anomalous-
diﬀusion term (see Sect. 5.2.4).

the frequency shift Ω 2 and the momentum-damping rate γ, which, as already
mentioned above, are independent of the temperature of the environment.
