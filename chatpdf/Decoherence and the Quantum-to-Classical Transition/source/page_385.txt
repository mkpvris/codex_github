lysis, can induce a change between the conformational states α and β of
a tubulin dimer located at one end of the microtubule [518]. Mediated by
dipole–dipole interactions between the dimers, it has been suggested that the
resulting change in the dipole moment associated with the dimer may then
propagate rapidly along the axis of the microtubule (see Fig. 9.7). Sataric,
