2.6 Which-Path Information
and Environmental Monitoring

Having laid out the key elements of the formalism and interpretation of
quantum mechanics relevant to decoherence—namely, quantum states, the
superposition principle, entanglement, density matrices, and the measure-
ment problem—we are now in an excellent position to ﬁnally approach our
actual subject of interest, namely, decoherence. We shall go about this task
by ﬁrst revisiting the well-known double-slit experiment, which will provide
us with a very intuitive and accessible illustration of the basic mechanism of
decoherence.

and Complementarity
