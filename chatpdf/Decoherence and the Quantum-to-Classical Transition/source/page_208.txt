5.2.5 Dynamics of Quantum Brownian Motion

Let us now return to the general form of the master equation (5.25) for quan-
tum Brownian motion (with the central system represented by a harmonic
oscillator) and illustrate the resulting time evolution of the system gener-
ated by this equation. These dynamics have been studied in detail by many
authors. We will here mostly follow the analysis given by Paz, Habib, and
Zurek [184].
    We shall take the initial wave function of the system to be the coherent
superposition

interest [184]. The ﬁrst situation is that of the superposition (5.64) of two
