    The mean velocities of the molecules passing through the apparatus can
be adjusted between about 90 m/s and 220 m/s by changing the position
of the molecular source such that the ﬁxed-height delimiter selects diﬀerent
portions of the beam, which follows a free-ﬂight parabola. These velocities
correspond to de Broglie wavelengths between 2 and 6 pm. The entire appa-
ratus is contained in a vacuum chamber to minimize collisions between the
C70 molecules and other particles.
