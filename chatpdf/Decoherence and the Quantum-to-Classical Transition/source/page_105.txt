    We also observe that, since the Hamiltonian (2.100) commutes with the
Pauli operator σ̂z of the system, the populations of the system are conserved
quantities under the action of this Hamiltonian. There is no exchange of
energy between the system and the environment, and therefore the interaction
with the environment can inﬂuence only the degree of coherence present
in the system. This model is therefore an example of decoherence without
dissipation, showing that decoherence is a pure quantum eﬀect without any
classical counterpart (see also Sect. 2.11).
