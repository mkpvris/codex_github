The assumption of bilinear coupling (linear in both X̂ and q̂i ) is an important
assumption of our model, which is therefore often explicitly referred to as
“linear quantum Brownian motion.” Our following discussion will be based
on this most commonly studied model.
    We see that the system–environment interaction is of the form (2.91),
which describes the continuous environmental monitoring of the position
coordinate of the system. Thus, without any further calculations, we can
already anticipate that the coupling (5.4) will, among other possible conse-
quences such as dissipative eﬀects and decoherence in other bases, lead to
decoherence of the system in the position basis.
