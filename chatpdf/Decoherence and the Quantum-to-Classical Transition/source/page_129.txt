– We emphasized that decoherence is a pure quantum-mechanical eﬀect
  without classical analog. In particular, decoherence does not need to be
  accompanied by dissipation. If it is, then decoherence typically happens
  on timescales vastly shorter than the timescales for dissipation (especially
  on macroscopic scales). Also, classical noise processes have to be clearly
  distinguished from decoherence, both from conceptual and observational
  points of view.
