emergence of classicality is to be tied not only to determinate properties at
each instant of time, but also to the existence of quasiclassical “trajecto-
ries” in property space. Since decoherence allows one to reidentify compo-
nents of the decohered density matrix over time, this could be used to derive
property states with a continuous, quasiclassical trajectory-like time evolu-
tion based on Schrödinger dynamics alone. For discussions of this approach,
see [432, 433].

8.3.2 Property Assignment Based on
Instantaneous Schmidt Decompositions

Since it is often rather diﬃcult to determine explicitly the robust pointer
states in more complicated models (see the predictability-sieve approach de-
scribed in Sect. 2.8.3), the problem arises of how to specify a simple yet
general rule for property assignment based on environment-induced supers-
election that is easy to apply to concrete cases of interest. To simplify this
situation, several modal interpretations have restricted themselves to the or-
thogonal decomposition of the density matrix to deﬁne the set of properties
that can be assigned (see, for instance, [68, 434–437]).
