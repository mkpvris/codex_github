    Conversely, the inability of writing a master equation in the Lindblad
form does not necessarily imply that the positivity condition (4.42) is vio-
lated. A good example is the model for quantum Brownian motion, which
we shall discuss in Sect. 5.2. For this model, it is possible to derive the ex-
act, non-Markovian master equation (see Sect. 5.2.7). This equation cannot
be brought into the Lindblad form without imposing several approximations
and assumptions [183]. Yet, it is clear that, since the master equation is ex-
act, it should automatically enforce the positivity of the density matrix at all
times. As it turns out, this is indeed the case. The explanation for this ini-
tially puzzling behavior can be found through a careful analysis of the exact
dynamics, which shows that in the exact master equation positivity arises
through rather subtle time dependences of the coeﬃcients appearing in the
master equation [184, 185].
