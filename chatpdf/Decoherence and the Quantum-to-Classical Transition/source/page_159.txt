Thus, without the environment, the spreading proceeds at exactly the same
rate in both the x = x direction (describing the ensemble of positions) and
the x = −x direction (representing spatial coherence). This, of course, is
the expected behavior for a free particle: If the environment is absent, there
exists no physical mechanism that would single out one of these two direc-

asymmetry is introduced. Now the scattering events act as a form of measure-
