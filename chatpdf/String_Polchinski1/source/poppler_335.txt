The vertex operator positions and conformal frames are not explicitly
denoted in eq. (9.6.12) but are as shown in figure 9.11. A z 2/3 mapping is
used to fit the three vertex operator half-disks into the disk. This action
passes an important check. The path integral on the disk is nonzero
only for insertions of total Qg = 3. The vertex operators for the physical
states all have ghost number 1, so both terms in the action (9.6.12) are
nonvanishing. Since the action (9.6.11) is invariant, we might expect that
for the gauge field it reduces to the non-Abelian Yang–Mills action, and
this is the case.
