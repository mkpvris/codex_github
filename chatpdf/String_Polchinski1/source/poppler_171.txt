  There is a further complication as there was for the point particle.
Requiring the metric to take the form (5.1.9) with τ in a given fundamental
region does not fix all of the di!×Weyl invariance. The metric and
periodicity are left invariant by rigid translations,
                                 σa → σa + va ,                          (5.1.16)
so this two-parameter subgroup of di!×Weyl is not fixed. In addition,
the discrete transformation σ a → −σ a leaves the metric invariant, and in
the unoriented case also (σ 1 , σ 2 ) → (−σ 1 , σ 2 ) with τ → −τ̄. Thus there are
again two kinds of mismatch between the metric degrees of freedom and
the local symmetries: parameters in the metric that cannot be removed
by the symmetries, and symmetries that are not fixed by the choice of
metric. The unfixed symmetries are known as the conformal Killing group
(CKG).

fix the gauge invariance by fixing some of the vertex operator positions.
