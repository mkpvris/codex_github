   It is worth noting that the intermediate steps in the path integral method
depend in a detailed way on the particular choice of Riemannian metric.
The metric is needed in order to preserve coordinate invariance in these
steps. A di!erent, Weyl-equivalent, metric gives a di!erent Laplacian and
di!erent eigenfunctions, though at the very end this dependence must
drop out in string theory. The second method uses only the basic data of
a Riemann surface, its holomorphic structure.
