At large x1 the exponential tachyon dominates the linear dilaton and the
path integral is suppressed, corresponding to an effective potential for the
string due to the tachyon background. Physically one has an asymptotic
region x1 → −∞, where the coupling goes to zero. Strings propagating
toward positive x1 interact with one another and reflect off the potential
and back to the asymptotic region. The maximum effective coupling is set
by the energy and the strength T0 of the potential. This picture, shown in
figure 9.12, is a bit exotic, but in the interest of finding a solvable model it
is worth pursuing. A model with rather similar physics, the static model,
played a role in unraveling the nature of quantum field theory.
