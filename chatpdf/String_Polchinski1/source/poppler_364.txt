  In order to follow the bosonic discussion as closely as possible, it is
useful to define states that are formally eigenstates of ψ̂:
                                       |ψ& = | ↓& + | ↑&ψ ,                             (A.2.7a)
                                     ψ̂|ψ& = |ψ&ψ .                                     (A.2.7b)
Define %ψ| to satisfy
                                       %ψ|ψ ! & = ψ − ψ ! .                               (A.2.8)
The right-hand side is the Grassmann version of the Dirac delta function.
It has the same properties as the ordinary delta function,
                             (ψ − ψ ! )f(ψ) = (ψ − ψ ! )f(ψ ! )                           (A.2.9)
and
                                 #
                                     dψ (ψ − ψ ! )f(ψ) = f(ψ ! )                        (A.2.10)

for arbitrary f(ψ). The state %ψ| is a left eigenstate with opposite sign,
                                       %ψ|ψ̂ = −ψ%ψ| ,                                  (A.2.11)
as one verifies by taking the inner product of both sides with a general
state |ψ ! &. Note also that
                                       #
                                           |ψ& dψ %ψ| = 1 .                             (A.2.12)

One can verify this by taking the matrix element between arbitrary states
%ψ ! | and |ψ !! & and evaluating the Grassmann integral.
  The delta function has an integral representation
                   #
               −       dχ exp[χ(ψ ! − ψ)] = ψ − ψ ! = %ψ|ψ ! & .                        (A.2.13)

By expanding the inner product (A.2.8) in powers of ψ ! one obtains
                                 %ψ| ↓& = ψ ,     %ψ| ↑& = −1 ,                         (A.2.14)
and therefore
                         %ψ|χ̂|ψ ! & = %ψ| ↑&
                                            #
                                      =−        dχ χ exp[χ(ψ ! − ψ)] .                  (A.2.15)
