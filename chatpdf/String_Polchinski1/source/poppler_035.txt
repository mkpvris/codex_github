The Weyl invariance, a local rescaling of the world-sheet metric, has no
analog in the Nambu–Goto form. We can understand its appearance by
looking back at the equation of motion (1.2.17) used to relate the Polyakov
and Nambu–Goto actions. This does not determine γab completely but
only up to a local rescaling, so Weyl-equivalent metrics correspond to the
same embedding in spacetime. This is an extra redundancy of the Polyakov
formulation, in addition to the obvious di! invariance.
