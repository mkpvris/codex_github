                       Expression in terms of determinants
In eq. (5.3.8) we have represented the Faddeev–Popov determinant as an
integral over Grassmann parameters and fields. We now reduce it to a
product of finite-dimensional and functional determinants. When we go
on to study the string S-matrix in coming chapters, this direct path integral
approach is just one of the methods we will use.
