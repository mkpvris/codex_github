                    9.2   Higher genus Riemann surfaces

faces. The one that will be most useful for our purposes is based on the
idea of building higher genus surfaces by sewing together lower genus
surfaces. This will be described in the next section, but first for com-
pleteness we describe some other common constructions. The treatment
is necessarily rather brief.
