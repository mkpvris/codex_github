   From this dimensional analysis one learns immediately that the quantum
gravitational correction is an irrelevant interaction, meaning that it grows
weaker at low energy, and in particular is negligible at particle physics
energies of hundreds of GeV. By the same token, the coupling grows
stronger at high energy and at E > MP perturbation theory breaks
down. In the two-graviton correction of figure 1.1(c) there is a sum over
