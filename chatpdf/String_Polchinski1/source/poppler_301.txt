                                      Open strings
In the case of open strings, the situation is similar. Let us focus for
convenience on a single compact dimension. Again there is one orientifold
fixed plane at 0 and another at πR ! . Introducing S O(n) Chan–Paton factors
(the symplectic case works in the same way), a general Wilson line can be
brought to diagonal form, and for n even the eigenvalues are paired:
                            !                                              "
              W = diag eiθ1 , e−iθ1 , eiθ2 , e−iθ2 , · · · , eiθn/2 , e−iθn/2 .   (8.8.4)

Thus in the dual picture there are 12 n D-branes on the line segment
0 ≤ X !25 ≤ πR ! , and 12 n at their image points under the orientifold
identification. Strings can stretch between D-branes and their images as
shown in figure 8.7. The generic gauge group is U(1)n/2 . As in the oriented
case, if r D-branes are coincident there is a U(r) gauge group. However, if
now the r D-branes in addition lie at one of the fixed planes, then strings
stretching between one of these branes and one of the image branes also
