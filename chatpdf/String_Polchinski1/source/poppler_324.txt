   It is interesting to consider again the boundary of moduli space, given
by the q → 0 limit of one or more handles. We see that the operators
of lowest weight will control the behavior. The results (9.4.6) and (9.4.7)
resemble the OPE, and in fact (9.4.6) reduces to the OPE when M2 is a
sphere and ‘. . .2 ’ is a pair of vertex operators. This analogy is especially
clear from figure 9.3(c), representing the limit as the insertion of a small
handle. The sewing relation shows that not only a product of operators,
but also a small handle, or any other disturbance that we might sew into
a small circle on the world-sheet, is equivalent to a sum of local operators.
