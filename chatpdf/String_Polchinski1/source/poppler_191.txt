  Both the disk and projective plane have been represented as the sphere
with points identified under a Z2 transformation, or involution. In fact,
every world-sheet can be obtained from a closed oriented world-sheet by
identifying under one or two Z2 s. The method of images can then be used
to obtain the Green’s functions.
