In                   this                   section                  we                  prove                  that                   the                  BRST                  cohomology                   of                   the                  string                   has
a                  positive                  inner                  product                  and                  is                  isomorphic                  to                  the                  light-cone                  and                  OCQ
spectra.            We            are            identifying            the            BRST            cohomology            as            the            physical            Hilbert
space.              We              will              also              need              to              verify,              in              our              study              of              string              amplitudes,              that
the          amplitudes          are          well          deﬁned          on          the          cohomology          (that          is,          that          equivalent
states              have              equal              amplitudes)              and              that              the              S-matrix              is              unitary              within              the
space             of             physical             states.
