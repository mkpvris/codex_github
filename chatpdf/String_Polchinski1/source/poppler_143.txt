                     4.1   Old covariant quantization

In conformal gauge, the world-sheet fields are X µ and the Faddeev–Popov
ghosts bab and ca . The Hilbert space is bigger than the actual physical
spectrum of the string: the D sets of αµ oscillators include unphysical
oscillations of the coordinate system, and there are ghost oscillators. As
is generally the case in covariant gauges, there are negative norm states
from the timelike oscillators (because the commutator is proportional to
the spacetime metric ηµν ), and also from the ghosts.

space, consider the amplitude on the infinite cylinder for some initial state
