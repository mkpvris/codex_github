  The simplest Poincaré-invariant action that does not depend on the
parameterization would be proportional to the proper time along the
world-line,
                                      !
                         Spp = −m          dτ (−Ẋ µ Ẋµ )1/2 ,              (1.2.2)

where a dot denotes a τ-derivative. The variation of the action, after an
integration by parts, is
                                            !
                             δSpp = −m          dτ u̇µ δX µ ,                (1.2.3)

where
                              uµ = Ẋ µ (−Ẋ ν Ẋν )−1/2                     (1.2.4)
is the normalized D-velocity. The equation of motion u̇µ = 0 thus describes
free motion. The normalization constant m is the particle’s mass, as can
be checked by looking at the nonrelativistic limit (exercise 1.1).
   The action can be put in another useful form by introducing an ad-

It will be convenient to work with the tetrad η(τ) = (−γττ (τ))1/2 , which
is defined to be positive. We use the general relativity term tetrad in any
number of dimensions, even though its root means ‘four.’ Then
