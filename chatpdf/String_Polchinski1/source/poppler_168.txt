we will define the path integral using a description like (5.1.5a), where
the fields are functions on a fixed coordinate region, and then convert to
a description like (5.1.5b) where the metric is fixed and the moduli are
encoded in the coordinate range.
  There is a second complication with the gauge-fixing. The condition
e = constant is preserved by the rigid translation
                               τ → τ + v mod 1 .                            (5.1.6)
That is, we have not said where on the circle to put the origin of the
coordinate system. Fixing the metric thus leaves a small part of the local
symmetry unfixed. So there is a small mismatch in both directions, metrics
that are not gauge-equivalent to one another and gauge transformations
that are not fixed by the choice of metric.
