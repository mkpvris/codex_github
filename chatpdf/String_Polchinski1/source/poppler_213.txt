              6.5 Chan–Paton factors and gauge interactions                         191

For the massless gauge bosons the Chan–Paton factors are the n × n
antisymmetric matrices and so the gauge group is S O(n). The states at
even mass levels transform as the adjoint representation of the orthogonal
group S O(n), and the states at odd mass levels transform as the traceless
symmetric tensor plus singlet representations.
  The oriented theory has a larger set of orientation-reversing symmetries,
obtained as a combination of Ω and a U(n) rotation,

implies that
