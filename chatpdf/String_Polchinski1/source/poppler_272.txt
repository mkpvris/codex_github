  Alternatively, consider the torus path integral for the partition function.
The antisymmetric tensor term is locally a total derivative and so depends
only on the topology of the configuration. Consider the configuration
                           X m = (w1m , 1 + w2m , 2 )R .                 (8.4.11)
The spatial direction on the torus winds w1m times around each periodic
spacetime dimension xm , and the timelike dimension w2m times. The Bmn
world-sheet action is
                                  2πibmn w1m w2n ,                       (8.4.12)
where bmn = Bmn R 2 /α% . One can check that the sum over path integral
sectors with phase (8.4.12) is related by Poisson resummation to the
partition function for the shifted spectrum (8.4.9).
