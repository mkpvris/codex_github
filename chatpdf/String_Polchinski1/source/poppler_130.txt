                        3.7         Strings in curved spacetime

Now we are ready to consider an important new issue, strings moving in
a curved spacetime. Let us recall first that the point-particle action has a
natural extension to curved spacetime. Replacing the flat metric ηµν with
a general metric Gµν (x) gives the action

This is a natural guess, but the reader might make the following objection.
We have learned that the graviton itself is one state of the string. A curved
spacetime is, roughly speaking, a coherent background of gravitons, and
therefore in string theory it is a coherent state of strings. Merely putting
a curved metric in the action (3.7.2) doesn’t seem stringy enough. To see
why it is justified, consider a spacetime that is close to flat,
