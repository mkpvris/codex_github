We can regard the additional insertions as preparing arbitrary initial and
final states (or we could do the same thing with boundary conditions).
The path integral statement (2.1.16) is thus the same as the statement in
the Hilbert space formalism that
                                            ∂∂¯X̂ µ (z, z̄) = 0                                  (2.1.17)
holds for all matrix elements of the operator X̂ µ (z, z̄). Thus we refer
to relations that hold in the sense (2.1.16) as operator equations. The
statement (2.1.17) is Ehrenfest’s theorem that the classical equations of
motion translate into operator equations.
