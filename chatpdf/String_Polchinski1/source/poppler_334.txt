Here, ϕ is the tachyon field and Aµ the gauge field, while B and C
are the Faddeev–Popov ghost fields associated with the spacetime gauge
symmetry. The primed fields can all be gauged away or are auxiliary
(that is, they satisfy algebraic rather than differential equations and can
be eliminated). One can then check that the symmetry (9.6.2) includes the
ordinary gauge transformation on Aµ , while the action is of Klein–Gordon
form for ϕ and of Maxwell form for Aµ . The BRST string field formalism
thus gives a very compact generalization of spacetime gauge invariance.
