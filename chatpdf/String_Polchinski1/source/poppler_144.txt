   In the remainder of this section we will impose the condition (4.1.2) in
a simple but somewhat ad hoc way. This is known as the old covariant
quantization (OCQ), and it is sufficient for many purposes. In the next
section we will take a more systematic approach, BRST quantization.
These are in fact equivalent, as will be shown in section 4.4.
