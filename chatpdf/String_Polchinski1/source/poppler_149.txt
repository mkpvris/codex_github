This is the essential condition: physical states must be BRST-invariant. We
have assumed that Q†B = QB . There are several ways to see that this must
indeed be the case. One is that if Q†B were di!erent, it would have to be
some other symmetry, and there is no candidate. A better argument is
that the fields cα and bA are like anticommuting versions of the gauge
parameter 1α and Lagrange multiplier BA , and so inherit their reality
properties.
