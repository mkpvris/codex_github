                                  The scalar partition function

coupling constant, but we cannot do this for the torus, in particular
because there is a nontrivial τ-dependence. In fact, we will see that there
is a great deal of physics in the amplitude with no vertex operators at all.

can think of the torus with modulus τ as formed by taking a field theory
on a circle, evolving for Euclidean time 2πτ2 , translating in , 1 by 2πτ1 ,
