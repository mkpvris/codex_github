In the interior, the equation of motion is as above. The boundary term
vanishes if
                          ∂σ X µ (τ, 0) = ∂σ X µ (τ, +) = 0 .                     (1.2.28)
These are Neumann boundary conditions on X µ . Stated more covariantly,
                             na ∂a X µ = 0         on ∂M ,                        (1.2.29)
where na is normal to the boundary ∂M. The ends of the open string
move freely in spacetime.
  The surface term in the equation of motion will also vanish if we
impose
             X µ (τ, +) = X µ (τ, 0) ,         ∂σ X µ (τ, +) = ∂σ X µ (τ, 0) ,   (1.2.30a)
             γab (τ, +) = γab (τ, 0) .                                           (1.2.30b)
That is, the fields are periodic. There is no boundary; the endpoints are
joined to form a closed loop.
