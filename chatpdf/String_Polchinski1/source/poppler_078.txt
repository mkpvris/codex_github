  Any CFT thus has an infinite set of conserved charges, the Virasoro
generators, which act in the Hilbert space and which satisfy the alge-
bra (2.6.19). Let us for now notice just a few simple properties. Generally
we work with eigenstates of L0 and L̃0 . The generator L0 satisfies
                              [L0 , Ln ] = −nLn .                       (2.6.20)
If |ψ, is an eigenstate of L0 with eigenvalue h, then
                L0 Ln |ψ, = Ln (L0 − n)|ψ, = (h − n)Ln |ψ, ,            (2.6.21)
so that Ln |ψ, is an eigenstate with eigenvalue h − n. The generators with
n < 0 raise the L0 eigenvalue and those with n > 0 lower it.
