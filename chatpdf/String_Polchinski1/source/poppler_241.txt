   The torus illustrates a general principle, which holds for all string
amplitudes: there is no UV region of moduli space that might give rise to
high energy divergences. All limits are controlled by the lightest states, the
long-distance physics. For the torus with vertex operators the τ-integration

operators approach one another. The same general principle applies to
