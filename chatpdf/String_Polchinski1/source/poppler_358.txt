  A minor but sometimes confusing point: if Â is a Hermitean operator,
the Euclidean Heisenberg operator Â(u) = exp(Ĥu)Â exp(−Ĥu) satisfies

                                 Â(u)† = Â(−u)                               (A.1.36)

and so is not in general Hermitean. For this reason it is useful to define a
Euclidean adjoint,

                                 Â(u) = Â(−u)† .                             (A.1.37)

A Hermitean operator in the Schrödinger picture becomes Euclidean
self-adjoint in the Euclidean Heisenberg picture.
