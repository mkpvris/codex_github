These two coordinate systems are shown in figure 2.3. In terms of the
w coordinate, time corresponds to translation of σ 2 = Im w. In terms of
z, time runs radially, the origin being the infinite past. These coordinates
are related by a conformal transformation. The w coordinate is natural
for the canonical interpretation of the theory, but the z coordinate is also
quite useful and most expressions are written in this frame.
