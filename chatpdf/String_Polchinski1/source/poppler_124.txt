                 Vertex operators in the Polyakov approach
We have in the state–operator mapping a systematic means of writing the
flat world-sheet vertex operator for any state. Since the world-sheet can
always be made locally flat, this is in principle all we need. For perspective,
however, it is useful to study the curved world-sheet vertex operators in
the Polyakov formalism. We will do this in the remainder of the section.
The method we use will be somewhat clumsy and not as systematic as
the state–operator mapping, but some of the results are useful. The reader
need not work out all the details.
