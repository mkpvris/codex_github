Notice that ω(σ) does not appear in the final form. This is because the co-
variant z̄-derivative of a tensor that has only z indices reduces to the simple
derivative, and vice versa, as the reader can check by working out the
connection tensor. We have positioned the indices, lower on b and upper
on c, to take advantage of this. Since the action (3.3.24) is Weyl-invariant,
it follows that bab and ca must be neutral under the Weyl transformation
(in contrast to bab and ca , for example, which contain additional factors
of the metric). Since the conformal transformation is a combination of a
coordinate transformation (3.3.7) and a Weyl transformation (3.3.9), we
can immediately read o! the conformal transformations of bab and ca
from the tensor indices: this is a bc CFT with (hb , hc ) = (2, −1) and a b̃c̃
CFT with (h̃b , h̃c ) = (2, −1).
