7.11 This exercise reproduces the first appearance of D = 26 in string the-
ory (Lovelace, 1971). Calculate the cylinder vacuum amplitude from the
Coleman–Weinberg formula in the form (7.3.9), assuming D spacetime di-
mensions and D% net sets of oscillators. Fix the constant in the open string
mass-squared so that the tachyon remains at m2 = −1/α% , as required by
conformal invariance of the vertex operator. Insert an additional factor of
exp(−α% k 2 s/2) as in eq. (7.4.10) to simulate a scattering amplitude. Show
that unless D = 26 and D% = 24, the amplitude has branch cuts (rather
than poles) in k 2 .
7.12 Repeat the previous exercise for the torus, showing in this case that

(Shapiro, 1972).
