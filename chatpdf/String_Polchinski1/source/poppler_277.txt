The coordinate transformations (8.4.32) act on the spacetime two-torus
just as the modular group acts on the world-sheet two-torus, generating
a P S L(2, Z) on τ and leaving ρ invariant. The antisymmetric tensor
shift (8.4.33) is ρ → ρ + N24,25 with τ invariant. A simultaneous T -duality
on X 24,25 acts as ρ → −1/ρ with τ invariant. Together the last two
transformations generate a second P S L(2, Z), acting on ρ. In addition,
duality on X 24 alone takes (τ, ρ) → (ρ, τ) and spacetime parity X 24 → −X 24
takes (τ, ρ) → (−τ̄, −ρ̄). The full T -duality group is then
                        P S L(2, Z) × P S L(2, Z)× Z22 .                 (8.4.38)
A further equivalence comes from world-sheet parity, which takes (τ, ρ) →
(τ, −ρ̄). It reverses the sign of the ◦ product.
   The moduli space is therefore two copies of the moduli space of the
torus, together with some discrete identifications. The kinetic terms are
proportional to

The modular-invariant combination d2 τ/τ22 was encountered in the dis-
cussion of the genus-zero amplitude. The continuous group P S L(2, R)
carries any τ in the upper half-plane into any other, and a U(1) subgroup
leaves each τ invariant. Thus the upper half-plane can be regarded as
P S L(2, R)/U(1), and the full moduli space is
                    P S L(2, R) × P S L(2, R)

           U(1) × U(1) × P S L(2, Z) × P S L(2, Z) × Z22
This is the same as the earlier (8.4.31). In particular, P S L(2, R)×P S L(2, R)
is locally the same as O(2, 2, R), in parallel to the fact that S U(2) × S U(2)
is locally the same as O(4, R).
