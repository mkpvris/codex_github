To be precise, these are only Laurent expansions if λ is an integer, which

Consider first the states that are annihilated by all of the n > 0 operators.
The b0 , c0 oscillator algebra generates two such ground states | ↓, and | ↑,,
with the properties
                  b0 | ↓, = 0 , b0 | ↑, = | ↓, ,                           (2.7.18a)
                  c0 | ↓, = | ↑, , c0 | ↑, = 0 ,                           (2.7.18b)
                  bn | ↓, = bn | ↑, = cn | ↓, = cn | ↑, = 0 ,     n>0.     (2.7.18c)
The general state is obtained by acting on these states with the n < 0
modes at most once each (because these anticommute). For reasons to
appear later it is conventional to group b0 with the lowering operators
and c0 with the raising operators, so we will single out | ↓, as the ghost
vacuum |0,. In string theory we will have a holomorphic bc theory and an
antiholomorphic b̃c̃ theory, each with λ = 2. The closed string spectrum
thus includes a product of two copies of the above.
