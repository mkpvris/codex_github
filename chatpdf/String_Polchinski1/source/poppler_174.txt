   The metric moduli correspond to the kernel (5.2.6) of P1T and the CKVs
to the kernel (5.2.7) of P1 . The Riemann–Roch theorem relates the number
of metric moduli µ = dim ker P1T and the number of CKVs κ = dim ker P1
to the Euler number χ,
                                             µ − κ = −3χ .                                   (5.2.9)
We will be able to derive this later in the chapter. For a closed oriented
surface −3χ = 6g − 6. This counting refers to real moduli, separating
complex moduli such as τ into their real and imaginary parts.
