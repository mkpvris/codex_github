   It is interesting to study the di!×Weyl redundancy of the metric at
the infinitesimal level. That is, we are looking for small variations of
the metric that are not equivalent to di!×Weyl transformations and so
correspond to changes in the moduli. We are also looking for small
di!×Weyl transformations that do not change the metric; these would
be infinitesimal elements of the CKG, called conformal Killing vectors
(CKVs). This will give further insight into the origin of the moduli and
the CKG.
